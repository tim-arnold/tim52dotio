# SEO Strategic Baseline — Firefly Tutoring

**Domain:** fireflytutoring.com  
**CMS:** Wix  
**Audit date:** 2026-07-18  
**Data source:** `data/baseline-data.md`, `data/full-keyword-inventory.md`

---

## Executive Summary

- The site ranks for 38 tracked keywords but only 1 is in the top 10 — the branded term "firefly tutors." Estimated organic traffic value is ~79 clicks/month ($791 equivalent).
- The authentic commercial footprint (Seattle tutoring + test prep keywords) is approximately 10–12 keywords totaling ~1,170 monthly searches, all ranked 13–38 in Google.
- The backlink profile has 38 referring domains but approximately 15–18 are spam/scrapers. The two legitimate editorial links (wallyhood.org, parentmap.com) are from Seattle neighborhood/parenting publications — the right type, but far too few.
- The domain has zero presence in AI/LLM citations (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, etc.) — a growing gap as AI-generated answers increasingly replace local search clicks.
- GSC data was not accessible; traffic numbers in this report are DataForSEO estimates only.

### TLD Analysis

No .edu or .gov links. The 7 .info domain links are all spam. The 5 .com.au links are all from willettonuniforms.com.au (an Australian uniform company) — all nofollow, likely scraped business data. The 5 legitimate links are .com and .org.

### Anchor Text

The dominant anchor is the bare URL "fireflytutoring.com" (27 of 58 backlinks) — the classic spam pattern. Legitimate editorial links use "Firefly Tutoring" (6 links) and contextual anchors like "summer math review." The "Fire Fly Tutoring" misspelling (8 links, all nofollow) comes from business directories using incorrect brand data.

## AI / LLM Mention Presence

Source: `ai_opt_llm_ment_top_domains`, `ai_opt_llm_ment_top_pages` | US/EN | 2026-07-18

**Zero presence.** fireflytutoring.com does not appear in DataForSEO's AI Overview / LLM response citation index for US/English queries.

This means when users ask ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, or similar tools "who are good tutors in Seattle" or "find a math tutor in Seattle," Firefly is not being cited. Competitors with richer content, more reviews, and stronger authority profiles are likely occupying those responses.

AI visibility is increasingly a discovery channel alongside traditional organic search. Key inputs to AI citation: topical authority (content depth on tutoring subjects), E-E-A-T signals (reviews, staff credentials, local mentions), and structured data. None of these are currently strong for this domain.

## SERP Feature Presence

Based on `serp_item_types` in ranked keyword data | 2026-07-18

| SERP Feature | Firefly Present? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Organic listing | ✓ | All keywords |
| Local Pack | ✗ | Not appearing in map pack for Seattle tutoring |
| Featured Snippet | ✗ | Not detected |
| People Also Ask | ✗ | Not appearing |
| Knowledge Panel | ✗ | Not detected |
| AI Overview | ✗ | Not detected |

The Local Pack is the most critical gap. "Seattle tutoring" and "tutor in seattle" both show a local_pack SERP feature in DataForSEO data — meaning Google is showing a map pack for these queries. Firefly is not in it. Appearing in the local pack for these terms would likely be the single highest-impact SEO improvement available.

Local Pack presence requires: verified Google Business Profile (GBP) with complete information, reviews, proximity signals, and local citations. GBP status was not checked in this audit — this is a priority first step.
